# The first version is scoped from a Jellyfin archive, and is movies only

ShowStream's first version is scoped from a functional archive of Jellyfin v12.4, not from a feature list written from memory. The archive covers eight areas a streaming MVP needs: users and access, libraries and scanning, metadata, browsing, home and search, playback, transcoding and streaming, audio and subtitle tracks, and administration. The maintainer prunes each to Keep, Later or Drop, and what is kept becomes ShowStream's functional design. The first version is movies, delivered by Phases 4 to 6. TV shows come right after it, in Phase 7, so they are neither archived nor designed now, but no design may rule them out. This puts the scope ahead of every open phase, and costs the time to archive and prune before Phase 4's kit and Phase 5 start. `docs/functional/README.md` holds the method, and `docs/PLAN.md` the order.
